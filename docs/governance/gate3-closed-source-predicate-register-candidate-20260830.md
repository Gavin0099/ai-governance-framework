## 2. Method and classification

The extraction order is:

```text
frozen source -> atomic predicate -> source-defined evidence standard
              -> applicability -> disposition
```

It is never reversed from a current mechanism weakness into a new predicate.
The register uses these dispositions:

- `VALIDITY`: the source expressly makes violation invalid, fail-closed, or a
  stop condition for the relevant run.
- `COUNTABILITY`: the source expressly controls whether evidence, an arm, a run,
  or a future result may count toward a decision.
- `CONSTRAINT`: required protocol behavior whose source does not uniquely state
  an invalidity or countability consequence.
- `LIMITATION`: a claim ceiling or interpretation boundary, not a blocker.
- `FUTURE_HARDENING`: expressly deferred work that is not a current blocker.
- `AMBIGUOUS_UNRESOLVED`: the closed sources do not uniquely determine the
  predicate, evidence standard, or consequence. The author does not resolve it.

`Historical` means the 2026-07 Gate 2 experiment. `Prospective` means a future
Gate 3 natural pilot, counted pair, or repeat. Historical task-specific pins do
not automatically bind a future run. Program-level requirements enter a future
repeat only through the explicit H1/P0 same-protocol carry-forward.
